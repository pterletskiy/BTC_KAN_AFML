CPCV Inner Architecture

How Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation, nested hyperparameter tuning, and model training work together inside the pipeline.

Part 1 CPCV Split Structure
The dataset is divided into N=6 time-ordered groups. For each split, k=2 groups are held out as the test set, producing C(6,2) = 15 unique splits and 5 backtest paths. Training samples whose Triple Barrier labels overlap with the test period are purged, and an embargo buffer adds additional protection.

Part 2 Nested Hyperparameter Tuning
Inside each of the 15 splits, tuning runs on the 4 training groups only. The training fold is subdivided into 3 inner Purged K-Folds with a 10-observation embargo. Optuna's TPE sampler explores the hyperparameter space, and the Median Pruner kills underperforming trials after each inner fold. Each split gets its own optimal parameters.
Step 1 Training Fold → Inner Purged K-Fold
The 4 training groups are split into 3 contiguous time blocks. For each inner fold, one block is validation and the rest are training, with 10 observations purged around boundaries.

Step 2 Optuna TPE Sampler
The Tree-structured Parzen Estimator models the hyperparameter space as two distributions: parameters that produced good results (l) and bad results (g). Each trial samples from l/g to maximize the Expected Improvement, concentrating exploration in promising regions rather than exhaustive grid search.
20–30 trials per model (configurable via n_trials parameter). Search spaces capped to prevent overfitting on small samples.
Step 2b Median Pruner
After evaluating each inner fold, the running mean log loss is reported. If a trial's intermediate result falls below the median of all completed trials, it is terminated early. This cuts wasted compute on clearly bad hyperparameter regions.
3 startup trials run without pruning to establish the median baseline.
↓
Output Per-Split Optimal Parameters
The trial with the lowest mean log loss across all 3 inner folds wins. Its parameters are applied to the module-level constants before model training begins for this split. Parameters may differ across splits, correctly simulating dynamic adaptation to changing market regimes.
Part 3 Model Training & Prediction
After tuning, the training fold is split 80/20 for model fitting and probability calibration. All 6 models are trained with the tuned parameters, calibrated via temperature scaling, then predict on the held-out test fold. Each model runs with multiple seeds to assess initialization sensitivity.

Temperature Scaling
A single parameter T is optimized on the 20% calibration set to minimize NLL: P = softmax(logits / T). Corrects overconfident/underconfident probabilities without changing model rankings.
Test Fold Prediction
Calibrated probabilities are used to predict on the 2 test groups (never seen during tuning or training). Predictions are stored per (model, split, seed) for downstream evaluation, path stitching, DSR, and PBO computation.
Total per split: 6 models × 2–3 seeds = 14 predictions. Across 15 splits: ~210 prediction entries feeding into 5 backtest paths.
